# Batch reviews — what the human ruled, verbatim

Read-only history, kept for the same reason the records beside them are: 13
rulings cite these words as their evidence, and a verdict recorded only outside
the Cortex does not exist. Never modified, only added.

Nothing writes new ones. The review-slot apparatus these came from — the packet
page, the scored members, the `-r<N>` sittings, the review-minute budget — was
retired on 2026-09-03; the human now rules in the session where the results are
read, and `scripts/cortex.py rule <phase> <verb> --body <file>` puts the words
into `rulings/`, which is the ledger of record.
